    About us

    KolBites is a Calcutta kitchen rooted in Sector V — feeding the tech parks, the night-shift desks, and true street-food lovers.

    Our menu is the food this city queues up for on its evening pavements: flaky parathas wrapped hot off the smoking cast-iron tawa, fiery chowmin tossed wok-hot the Tangra way, crisp singaras, and spiced earthen-pot cha brewed slow.

    From the iron silhouette of the Howrah Bridge to the honk of a classic yellow Ambassador, Kolkata's soul lives in its flavours. We bring those authentic roadside counters together under one roof — cooked fresh, spiced bold, and served piping hot.
